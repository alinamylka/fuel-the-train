# Privacy Policy – Fuel the Train

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

Fuel the Train is a personal, non-commercial nutrition planning app developed and operated by Alina Mylka. It plans meals around training load and is currently used only by its developer.

## What data we collect

When you connect your Garmin account, Fuel the Train receives the following data through the Garmin Connect API:

- **Activity data:** activity type, date, duration, distance, calories, power, heart rate and training load.
- **Health data** (if you allow it): daily calories burned, sleep, heart rate variability and body weight.

The app also stores data you enter yourself: body weight, FTP, nutrition goals, favorite foods and meals you have eaten.

## How we use your data

Your data is used only to:

- calculate your daily energy and macronutrient needs based on your training,
- generate meal plans and meal suggestions,
- compare planned and actual intake.

To generate meal plans, relevant parts of your data (for example training summaries and food preferences) are sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic) for processing. This data is not used to train AI models.

## How we share your data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (hosting and AI processing), and only to the extent required.

## Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server and protected by access controls. Access credentials for connected services are stored encrypted.

## Your rights

You can disconnect your Garmin account at any time in Garmin Connect or in the app. After you disconnect, no new data is received. You can request that all of your data be deleted at any time, and it will be removed within 30 days.
